Questions

What is Mowa?

Mowa is an AI career companion for Android, built around a different question than most AI tools ask: not “how do I get more capable,” but “how do I stay focused enough to use the capability I already have.” It remembers context about you across sessions, breaks long-term goals into a daily arc, and reaches out proactively instead of waiting for you to ask.

How is Mowa different from ChatGPT or a to-do app?

Mowa is less of a chatbot, an almighty intelligent AI, or a productivity app, and more of a career companion there for the long haul. Think of it as if ChatGPT merged with your to-do app. You don't have to be the one to remind it to keep you productive, because it reaches out to you.

Who is it for?

Young people trying to find their direction, people who have one and need help staying on it, and anyone with days when they're tired, burnt out or confused in the midst of the grind. In practice: students juggling school and a side hustle, early-career professionals, and job seekers trying to stay consistent through a long search.

Is Mowa a therapist or a replacement for a career coach?

No. Mowa is a career companion. It helps you plan, follow through and think things over, and many people find it easy to talk to, but it isn't a mental-health service or a human mentor.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer. There is no iPhone version at the moment.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes. Mowa's AI runs on servers, so you need to be online to chat and to get new plans.

Is it free?

Mowa has a free tier with usage limits, and a paid plan with higher limits.

What happens to my data?

Your account, chats, plans and memory are tied to your profile and used to personalise Mowa for you. You can delete your account from the Profile screen in the app or by email. Read the privacy policy and the data deletion page.
